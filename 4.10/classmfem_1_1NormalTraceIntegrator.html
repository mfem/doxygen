Detailed Description

Integrator for the form: \( \langle v, w \cdot n \rangle \) over a face (the interface) where the trial variable \(v\) is defined on the interface ( \(H^{1/2}\), i.e., trace of \(H^1\)) and the test variable \(w\) is in an \(H(div)\)-conforming space.

Detailed Description

Inner product weighted by operators, X and Y. The method Eval() computes the inner product of two vectors, x and y, weighted by the operators, X and Y, as (Y(y),X(x)).

Compute the inner product (Y(y),X(x)) of vectors x and y, weighted by the operators X and Y.

Note
Either (but not both) operator, X or Y, can be set as null, in which case the corresponding vector is unmodified (i.e. the operator acts as an identity operator).
